A warning label for a time machine, written for make.fun's "Funniest Time Machine Warning Label" bounty: one line, 120 characters at most, phrased like a genuine safety notice — until the last few words, where the paradox quietly moves in and starts charging rent.

## The line

> WARNING: Do not wave at your past self. He waves back, follows you home, and now two of you are paying rent.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one line and writes the same line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

It turns a real hazard of time travel — meeting a duplicate of yourself — into the joke instead of naming it flatly, keeps the straight face of an official safety notice all the way through, and saves the surprise for the end: the wave is harmless, the consequence is a second tenant. Every phrase does work inside the 120-character limit, and the laugh lands where the reader least expects it.
